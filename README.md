# Jacobos-Store

## Audience
The project is aimed at the owner of the retail store called Jacobo’s Store.

## Problem
Jacob started his business in 2022 with high expectations; however, he acknowledges that his records have missing data, and as a result, he does not have enough information to understand the state of his business.

## Objectives
- Maximize sales
- Increase inventory turnover
- Improve supply chain management
- Active omnichannel strategy
- Customer loyalty
- Understand demand
- Payment methods

## Goal / Action
Have the owner approve a one-month pilot program featuring fixed promotional days for Item 8 products—which generate 80% of profits—with discounts of up to 50%, focusing on weekends with special Item 8 bundles alongside other lower-selling items.

## Benefits
The biggest benefit for Jacobo’s Store is that it builds customer loyalty while maximizing sales of the most popular items.

## Risks
The risks facing Jacobo’s Store are as follows:
- Failing to retain repeat customers
- Failing to increase sales by 8% by 2025
- Continuing to invest in slow-moving products
- Losing sales due to a lack of modernization and digital competitiveness.

## Great idea
We should approve the pilot program featuring seven days of promotions, with the strongest promotions running from Friday through Sunday, leveraging sales of our seven best-selling products to boost overall sales, achieving 8% growth compared to 2024, and reducing the number of slow-moving items.
